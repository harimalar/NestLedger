A calmer daily ledger.

NestLedger helps you record income and expenses, plan your month and understand spending on iPhone and iPad.

What’s in version 1.1

Three focused tabs — Ledger, Plan and Insights. Quick category entry, remembered notes, local receipts with text recognition, upcoming bills, savings goals, monthly history and Home Screen widgets.

No account, advertising or tracking SDKs. Version 1.1 removes the Pro purchase requirement and transaction limit. Your active ledger is stored locally; automatic iCloud sync is not included.

Keep a backup

Open Settings → Preferences & data → Backup & Export to save a JSON backup. CSV and PDF are useful for sharing or reports. Exported files are not encrypted by NestLedger: save them somewhere trusted. Importing a JSON backup replaces the active ledger.

Automatic iCloud backup update

Build 26 adds optional automatic iCloud Drive backups and a 9 PM check-in notification. This feature starts with version 1.1 build 26; builds through 25 do not include it. On build 26, open Settings → iCloud & backup to enable it. iOS controls background timing, so 9 PM is a checkpoint, not guaranteed upload completion. Check the confirmed upload status before deleting the app.

Uploaded backups remain in your iCloud Drive after app deletion. Use the same Apple Account, then choose Restore from iCloud during setup or Find iCloud backups in Settings. Restore replaces your ledger; it does not merge devices. Deleting the app removes local backups, including Restore Last Local Backup files. Keep an independent JSON export if using an earlier build.

Planning estimates

Safe to spend and daily pacing use your entries and configured budget. They are estimates, not bank balances or financial advice. Recording a bill payment does not send money to a biller. Always review receipt recognition suggestions.

Need help?

Email rajhari@gmail.com with your app version, device model, iOS version and the steps that caused the problem. Please avoid sending sensitive receipts or full ledger backups.

Manage your information

Edit or delete entries in the app. Settings → Preferences & data includes archive and reset controls. Remove exported and archived copies separately. Read the Privacy Policy for details and the Terms & Conditions for usage information.
