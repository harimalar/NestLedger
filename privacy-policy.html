NestLedger Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026. This policy describes NestLedger version 1.1 and later versions that link to it. Developer and support contact: Malar Hari, rajhari@gmail.com.

Your ledger stays under your control

NestLedger is a personal expense and income tracker. It requires no account and does not connect to banks. Transactions, amounts, notes, categories, budgets, bills, savings goals, receipt images and remembered entry suggestions are processed and stored on your device. The developer does not receive your ledger through the app. There are no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Local storage, backups and sharing

Build 26 adds optional automatic backups to your own iCloud Drive using Apple’s document storage. Enabling Save to iCloud uploads copies of your entries, notes, receipts, budgets, bills and goals to the Apple Account signed in on your device. The developer does not receive these files. This is backup and explicit restore, not live merging between devices. Earlier builds do not include automatic iCloud backup. Apple's device backup services may separately include app data according to your device settings. You can also choose to save or share a backup through Files, iCloud Drive, email or another provider using the system share sheet. Those services handle the files under their own privacy terms.

JSON backups and archives can contain your complete ledger, including notes and receipts. CSV and PDF exports contain the information shown in the export. These files are not password protected or encrypted by NestLedger. Keep them in a trusted location and share them only with intended recipients. App Lock does not protect copies exported outside the app. The app keeps local backup and archive files until you remove them; deleting the app removes those local copies. Uploaded iCloud copies survive app deletion and remain until you remove them in Files. Turning backup off or resetting the active ledger does not remove existing separate copies. Restore explicitly replaces your active ledger and first saves a local recovery copy. Automatic backups update a daily file for each installation; prior days and other installations are kept separately. iOS controls background scheduling, so a requested 9 PM backup is not guaranteed to execute at that exact time. Check the last confirmed upload status before deleting the app.

Camera, photos and receipt text

Camera access is requested when you photograph or scan a receipt. Photo selection uses Apple's system picker so you choose which images to provide. NestLedger compresses receipt images for local storage. Optional text recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework; receipts are not sent to an AI service. Recognized amounts, dates and notes are suggestions you review before saving.

App Lock, widgets and notifications

Optional App Lock uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework with the authentication methods available on your device. NestLedger receives the authentication result, not your face, fingerprint or device passcode. Your device security settings also affect protection of local data.

If you add a widget, a small budget summary is shared locally with the NestLedger widget extension. Notes and receipts are not included. The widget hides financial figures when App Lock or Hide Amounts is enabled, although system-rendered previews may take time to refresh. Remove the widget if you do not want a summary visible on your Home Screen.

Optional reminders are scheduled locally through iOS. Notification previews may show a bill name and amount. Control notification permissions and previews in iOS Settings. Daily backup check-ins and completed-upload notifications contain no financial amounts or notes. A scheduled check-in is not proof that a backup completed; upload confirmation is shown separately. No push-notification server is used.

Support and diagnostics

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the contents and attachments you send through your email provider. This is optional; avoid sending full backups, account numbers or sensitive receipts. We use correspondence to answer requests, troubleshoot and meet applicable obligations, retaining it only as reasonably needed for those purposes. Contact the support address to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

The app does not automatically upload diagnostic logs to the developer. Apple may provide crash or usage information according to your Apple sharing settings and policies. Local error messages may be recorded by the operating system. Any diagnostics you voluntarily send to support are handled as support correspondence.

Websites and service providers

Opening the support or policy website uses your browser. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site does not add analytics or advertising scripts. Apple services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Edit or delete individual entries in the app, or use Settings → Preferences & data → Data archive & reset to clear the active ledger. Undo and legacy recovery copies may temporarily retain deleted information. To remove the app's local storage completely, delete the app rather than offloading it; separately delete any backups, archives, exports and device backups you want removed. Copies already shared with another person or service must be managed there. The developer cannot recover, read or delete a ledger that exists only on your device.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy of information, or complain to a privacy regulator. Contact support for information we actually hold, such as correspondence; local ledger controls are described above. No account registration is required to exercise local controls.

Children and policy updates

NestLedger is a general-purpose personal recordkeeping tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly seek children's personal information through support. A parent or guardian may contact us about information sent by a child.

Changes to data handling will be reflected here with an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the app or release information, and consent will be requested where required. No software can guarantee absolute security; use a device passcode and keep independent backups of important records.
